About

The Silicon Hills Project is a not-for-profit, non-dilutive builderspace at the University of Texas at Austin, founded by Shreshth Saride, Eeshwar Parasuramuni, and Jaden George.

We operate independently of the University and aim to accelerate startups, incubate exceptional talent, and provide opportunities for the most ambitious students across Austin. We are where the best in Texas collide to change the world.
